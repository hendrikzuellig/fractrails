# FracTrails privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-08

FracTrails makes posters from your own activity data. Everything happens on your device.

## What the app collects

Nothing. FracTrails has no account, no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising. The app sends no data to the developer or to any third party.

## What stays on your device

- The activity files you import (a Strava bulk export, or GPX and FIT files) and the catalog the app builds from them.
- The posters you render.
- Your settings and collections.

These files live in the app's own container. They are included in your device backup, except for copies the app can rebuild. Deleting the app deletes them.

## Network use

FracTrails uses the network only to show Apple Maps in the live map. Apple's map service receives the map tiles it needs to draw the view, under Apple's privacy terms. The app sends no activity data to it. Help links open in your browser.

## Photos

When you save a poster to Photos, the app asks for permission to add to your library. It never reads your library.
