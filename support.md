# FracTrails support

FracTrails turns your activity data into posters: a grid, rings or a fan of every route, as PNG or SVG. There is no account and no sign-in. Everything stays on your device.

## First launch

The app opens with sample runs around New York, so you can try every feature before you import anything. Every import is a file you pick in the Files app. Your import replaces the samples. To get the samples back, open Settings and choose **Delete my data…**: your activities, posters and collections are deleted, the app starts over as after a fresh install, and FracTrails Pro stays.

## Get your data in

### From Strava

You ask Strava for your archive once. It takes 2 minutes, then a few hours of waiting.

1. Open your [Strava account page](https://www.strava.com/athlete/delete_your_account) in a web browser and log in.
2. Press **Request your archive**. Do not delete your account.
3. Wait for the e-mail from Strava. This can take a few hours. Meanwhile, try FracTrails with the
   sample runs around New York; your import replaces them.
4. Open the link in the e-mail. On an iPhone use Safari: it saves `export_….zip` to
   Files ▸ Downloads.
5. Back in FracTrails, press **Import…** in the sample-runs banner, then **Import my export…**,
   and pick the zip. You can also share the zip from Files to FracTrails, or open Settings and
   choose **Import Strava export…**.

No Strava account at hand? [sample-export.zip](sample-export.zip) is a synthetic export in the same format, 240 activities around San Francisco; import it the same way.

Strava's own help: [Exporting your data and bulk export][strava-export].

[strava-export]: https://support.strava.com/hc/en-us/articles/216918437-Exporting-your-Data-and-Bulk-Export

### GPX and FIT files

GPX and FIT files work too. Save them to the Files app, then open Settings and choose **Import GPX or FIT files…**.

## Make a poster

Pick the activities in the filter bar (sport, period, region, distance), open Create (a tab on iPhone and iPad, a toolbar button on the Mac), choose the layout, the colour and the labels, then press Create. The poster opens when it is ready; share it or save it to Photos.

## FracTrails Pro

Full HD and 4K posters, PNG and SVG, and all layouts are free. FracTrails Pro, a one-time purchase, adds high-resolution outputs: posters at 8K and A2 print resolution. To get it back on a new device, open Settings and choose Restore Purchases.

## What the app uses

- Apple Maps draws the live map. No other map service is used.
- The App Store handles the FracTrails Pro purchase.
- Nothing else: no server, no account, no analytics, no advertising. Your files are read on the device and stay there. See the [privacy policy](privacy).

The app works the same in every country and region. It is in English.

FracTrails is an independent app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Strava.
